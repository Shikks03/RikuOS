Specimen 03 · Choice

Hero row: two variants

Both rows carry today's real numbers at the true content width, stacked so that both drafts cards are in one view. The first is the ruled row: no graphic anywhere except the 4px proportion track on the contacts card, which draws 25-of-30 as a shape the figure does not state. The second adds one mark per draft to the drafts card — 24 marks, bleeding off the lower and right edges — and changes nothing else.

Question 1 for Riku

Bare, or the mark field? The argument for bare is that a field of 24 marks says what the 34px 24 already said, louder. The argument for the marks is that the bare row may read as a template. This is the one ruling whose cost only shows in pixels.

Specimen 05 · Re-skin

The queue page inside the shell

The existing /queue markup, unchanged, wearing the new stylesheet: every treatment here comes from one rule per selector — .card, .badge, .meta, pre.body, bare button, .secondary, .danger — so no TSX is touched. Scroll right for the same page with its inline header deleted — and with it the second Settings link, the RikuOS — Queue heading, and the second Log out. One cost is worth naming rather than discovering later: with their solid active state gone, the six status filters now differ only by border and label brightness, so the row reads as one strip until you look. That is the price of one treatment per selector, not a defect.

Question 3 for Riku

Keep or delete the duplicated header? In the left frame the inline Settings link and the rail's Settings nav item sit in the same eyeful, and RikuOS — Queue puts the app name and the page name at display weight under a rail that already carries both. The two Log out buttons are the smallest of the three: they are ~300px apart and rarely on screen together. Deleting the ten inline lines on queue and settings is the one place where “no TSX change” and “the shell works” genuinely conflict. The recommendation is delete.

A — as the page is today: rail plus its own inline header
